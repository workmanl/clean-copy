"Download selected removed" removes the checked blocks without re-encoding image data. Removing orientation or color information can change how an image displays. "Re-encode copy" draws the image through a canvas and exports a new PNG or JPEG, which can change quality, color, transparency or animation. Browser encoders may add basic format metadata. This is not a complete metadata audit or a watermark remover. Content Credentials record provenance; keep an original and preserve attribution and required disclosures when sharing.
