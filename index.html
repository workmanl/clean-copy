Clean Copy

Inspect supported metadata blocks in JPG, PNG and WebP images and MP4 and MOV videos, then choose which blocks to remove. Selected removal preserves compressed image and video data without re-encoding. Your files stay in your browser.

"Download selected removed" removes the checked blocks without re-encoding image data. Removing orientation or color information can change how an image displays. "Re-encode copy" draws the image through a canvas and exports a new PNG or JPEG, which can change quality, color, transparency or animation. Browser encoders may add basic format metadata. For MP4 and MOV videos, checked blocks are overwritten with empty padding in place, so the audio and video data are not changed. This is not a complete metadata audit or a watermark remover.
